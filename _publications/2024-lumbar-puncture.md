An image guidance framework for lumbar puncture that registers pre-operative MRI with intraoperative ultrasound to improve visualization of the spine and localize a safe needle insertion region. The pipeline combines intensity-based segmentation and 3D reconstruction for real-time ultrasound integration, improving registration accuracy by 13.2% IoU across 44 dynamic test cases. This work won the People's Choice Award at WPI's Graduate Research Innovation Exchange (GRIE) 2024.
